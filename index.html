project xcalibarn

A first-person action game where movement is part of the fight. Slide, dash, and carry momentum through a dark world marked by flowers.

The prototype explores shooting and a katana you can throw to open new routes. I'm building it solo, one mechanic at a time, as the game finds its shape.
